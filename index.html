Athens · porcelain & stoneware · since the first clay print in 2018

BinaryCeramics

Forms shaped by algorithm, made real in clay. BinaryCeramics is my studio in Athens. I design ceramic forms as systems of rules, and I design and build the printers and extruders that make them.

“Binary” is the zero and one behind the code, and also the tension the work lives in: digital and physical, machine precision and material plasticity, control and emergence.

101 About

An unusual hybrid of design, craft and science.

That's how a professor once described me, and it's still the most accurate description I have. I studied fine arts in Rome, where I built my first 3D printer from a kit in 2012. Since then I've worked across art, universities and tech in San Francisco, Berlin and Barcelona, building sensors, robots, plotters and installations, before clay brought me back to Athens.
