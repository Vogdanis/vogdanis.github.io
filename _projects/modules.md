Four triangular containers folded from copper sheet, each a different shape. They connect to each other with magnets, so they can be closed into a single band, opened into a star, stacked or interlocked into a new volume each time.

The raw pink copper was coloured with heat and patina into deep reds, rust, gold and violet, and every arrangement shows a different combination of their surfaces.
